## 1. Centrelink / Services Australia Payments

These are **federal government payments** you can claim through Services Australia (Centrelink).

### Family Tax Benefit (FTB) — Parts A & B
- **Free** 2-part payment to help with the cost of raising children
- Part A is paid per child based on family income
- Part B provides extra help for single-parent families or families with one main income

### Parenting Payment
- Main income support payment while you are a young child's primary carer
- Available as **Parenting Payment Single** or **Parenting Payment Partnered**

### Parental Leave Pay
- Up to **18 weeks** of pay while you care for a newborn or newly adopted child
- Paid at the national minimum wage rate

### Child Care Subsidy (CCS)
- Helps cover the cost of approved child care
- **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children** were entitled to at least **36 hours of subsidised child care per fortnight** (check current rules as this may have been updated from January 2026)

### Additional Child Care Subsidy
- Extra financial help on top of CCS for families experiencing hardship, including:
  - Grandparent carers
  - Families experiencing temporary financial hardship
  - Families transitioning from income support to work

### Newborn Upfront Payment & Newborn Supplement
- Lump sum + fortnightly supplement for newborns (if not receiving Parental Leave Pay)

### Remote Area Allowance
- Extra financial help if you live in a **remote area** and already receive an income support payment

### Rent Assistance
- Additional payment on top of your family payment if you rent privately

### Health Care Card / Pensioner Concession Card
- Provides access to cheaper medicines and bulk-billed doctor visits

### How to Apply
- **Online:** MyGov / Centrelink online account
- **Phone:** Centrelink Indigenous Call Centre (see contacts below)
- **In person:** Visit a Services Australia / Centrelink office — ask for an Indigenous Service Officer

## 2. Healthcare (Free)

### Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Health Check (Item 715)
- **Free** annual health assessment (every 9–12 months) for all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people
- Available for children from birth
- Checks growth, development, hearing, vision, immunisations, and social/emotional wellbeing
- Bulk-billed through Medicare — no out-of-pocket cost

### Closing the Gap PBS Co-Payment Program
- **Reduces or eliminates the cost of medicines** for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people with (or at risk of) chronic disease
- With a concession card, most medicines are **free**
- Ask your doctor or Aboriginal Health Worker to register you

### Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Services (ACCHS)
- **Free** or bulk-billed health services run by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities
- Culturally safe medical care, dental, mental health, maternal health, and child health services
- Find your local ACCHS through your regional Aboriginal Medical Service

### Queensland Health — First Nations Health
- All Queensland Hospital and Health Services are required to implement **health equity strategies** for First Nations peoples
- Free public hospital services
- Maternal and child health programs targeting Closing the Gap outcomes
- Goal: healthy birthweight target of 91% by 2031

### Key Free Health Services for Your Child
- Free immunisations (National Immunisation Program)
- Free hearing and vision checks
- Free developmental assessments
- Free dental care through the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (up to $1,095 over 2 years for eligible children)
- Bulk-billed GP visits at Aboriginal Medical Services

## 3. Childcare & Early Childhood

### Free Kindergarten
- **15 hours per week, 40 weeks per year** — FREE for all Queensland children at government-approved kindergarten programs
- **State Delivered Kindergarten** programs available in selected rural, remote, and Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities at local state schools

### Deadly Kindies Program
- Free **Deadly Kindies Kit** for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children who:
  - Are up to date with their 715 health check, AND
  - Are enrolled in a Queensland Government-approved kindergarten

### Child Care Subsidy (see Centrelink section above)
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children receive enhanced access to subsidised childcare hours

### Early Years Places
- Integrated service hubs for families with children aged **birth to 8 years**
- Free access to playgroup, early childhood education, health services, and parenting support — all in one location

### Connected Beginnings
- Australian Government community-led grants program
- Supports Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children to get the best start in life through integrated early childhood and family support services

## 4. Education & Schooling

### Free State School Education
- All Queensland state schools are free (Prep to Year 12)
- Schools automatically receive Indigenous education funding — no application needed

### ABSTUDY
- **Federal government payment** specifically for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students
- Available for:
  - Primary and secondary students
  - Tertiary students
  - Apprentices
- Can include:
  - Living allowance
  - School fees allowance
  - Travel allowance (including for students at boarding school)
  - Incidentals allowance (books, equipment)
  - Away-from-home entitlements

### Indigenous Student Success Program (ISSP)
- University-level support program funded by NIAA
- Provides tutoring, mentoring, and financial assistance for Indigenous university students

### Scholarships
- **Pearl Duncan Teaching Scholarship** — Up to $20,000 over 4 years + guaranteed permanent teaching position at a Queensland state school
- Various other Indigenous-specific scholarships through universities and TAFE

### TAFE Queensland — Indigenous Support
- Free or subsidised TAFE courses for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students
- ABSTUDY assistance available
- **RATEP Program** — Pathway to become a registered teacher or qualified teacher aide

## 5. Family Support Services

### Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Family Wellbeing Services
- **Free and confidential** one-stop-shop for families
- Delivered by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander community-controlled organisations
- Covers: social, emotional, physical, and spiritual wellbeing
- Includes specialist workers for:
  - **Youth at risk** (Indigenous Youth and Family Workers)
  - **Domestic and Family Violence** (Specialist DFV Workers)
- Available across Queensland

### Family and Child Connect
- **Free** service — call **13 FAMILY (13 32 64)**
- Helps connect families with local support services
- Can arrange an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander family support worker
- Free interpreters available

### Family Participation Program
- Helps Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander families **participate in child protection decisions**
- Families lead the process and develop plans to keep children safe

### SMS4DeadlyDads
- **Free** text message service for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander fathers
- Regular tips about connecting with your baby, supporting your partner, and self-care

### Triple P — Positive Parenting Program
- **Free** parenting program for Queensland parents
- Helps raise happy, healthy children and teenagers

### QATSICPP — Queensland Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Child Protection Peak
- Peak advocacy body for Indigenous child protection and family support
- Can connect you with local member organisations across Queensland

## 6. Housing

### Social Housing
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander families can apply for **public housing** and **community housing** through the Queensland Government
- Rent is capped at a percentage of household income (usually 25%)
- Priority access may be available for families with children

### Rent Assistance (Centrelink)
- Additional payment if you are renting privately and receiving a Centrelink payment

### Bond Loan
- Interest-free loan from the Queensland Government to help pay your rental bond

### Remote Home Ownership Program
- Available in discrete Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities
- **Rent-to-buy schemes** (e.g., Palm Island scheme — up to 300 homes)
- 99-year home ownership leases available

### First Nations Housing and Homelessness Partnership
- Queensland Government partnership to improve housing outcomes
- Minimum **$290 million** dedicated investment
- 20-year housing plan under development

### Community Housing Providers
- **Home in Place** — Not-for-profit provider with dedicated Aboriginal housing in South East Queensland
- **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Housing Queensland (ATSIHQ)** — Advocacy organisation

### If You're at Risk of Homelessness
- Contact **13 FAMILY (13 32 64)** or visit your local Centrelink office immediately
